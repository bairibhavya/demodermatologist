# Photography and visual assets

The website primarily references Unsplash-hosted editorial stock photos at runtime, with local fallbacks cropped from the original AI-generated approved design montage in `docs/APPROVED-VISUAL-DIRECTION.png`.

Photographs used are illustrative only. No depicted model represents a clinic patient, clinician, result, or facility. The approved mockup includes invented display data; this implementation avoids falsely attributing verified statistics or medical qualifications.

Before commercial use, obtain clinic-owned images or check each photo's license and attribution requirements. For actual patient before/after photography, use documented informed consent and compliant handling. Check all remote image URLs from the deployment location, and replace any broken links.
